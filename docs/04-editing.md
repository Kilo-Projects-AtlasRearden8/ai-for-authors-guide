# Editing with AI

First drafts are supposed to be messy. Editing is where a book becomes readable. AI is a good assistant here — it can spot patterns you miss — but you make the final calls.

## What AI is good at in editing

- Spotting repeated words and phrases
- Finding long, rambling sentences
- Suggesting tighter phrasing
- Checking dialogue tags
- Noticing timeline or character inconsistencies (in small chunks)

## What AI is not good at

- Knowing your voice better than you do
- Catching everything (it misses things too)
- Understanding emotional nuance
- Editing a whole novel at once (work in small chunks)

## Step 1: Work in small chunks

Never paste your whole manuscript (Chapter 02). Edit one chapter or one scene at a time.

> **Privacy reminder:** Never paste your unpublished manuscript into a web AI chat. Work with one chapter at a time, or use a made-up sample scene (see Chapter 02).

## Step 2: Check for repeats

**Try this prompt:**
> "I am editing one chapter of my novel. Find words or phrases I repeat too often. List the top 5 repeats and suggest alternatives. Do not rewrite the chapter. Here is the chapter: [paste one chapter]"

Review the list. Decide which repeats to cut. You know your style better than AI does.

## Step 3: Tighten sentences

**Try this prompt:**
> "Find sentences in this chapter that are long or rambling. Suggest shorter versions. Keep my tone. Do not change the meaning. Here is the chapter: [paste one chapter]"

Read each suggestion. Keep what works. Ignore what does not.

## Step 4: Polish dialogue

**Try this prompt:**
> "Read this dialogue. Flag any lines that sound unnatural, too formal, or like all characters sound the same. Suggest fixes. Do not rewrite the whole scene. Here is the chapter: [paste one chapter]"

Read dialogue out loud. If it sounds wrong when you say it, fix it.

## Step 5: Check consistency in a scene

**Try this prompt:**
> "In this scene, check for inconsistencies: a character changing clothes mid-scene, a door that is open then closed without explanation, or a person knowing something they should not know. Flag only real problems. Here is the scene: [paste one scene]"

## Step 6: Make the final decision

AI suggests. You decide. If a suggestion makes your writing worse, ignore it. Your name is on the cover.

## Editing exercise

1. Copy one chapter into a local file (not an AI chat yet).
2. Open your AI tool. Paste the chapter (only if you are comfortable per Chapter 02; otherwise use a made-up sample scene).
3. Run Steps 2–4 above.
4. Apply the changes you agree with. Save a new version of the chapter.

## Remember

- Never paste your full unpublished manuscript into a web AI chat.
- Edit in small chunks.
- You are the editor. AI is the assistant.
