# Privacy and Safety

This is the most important chapter in this guide. Read it before you paste anything into any AI tool.

## The golden rule

**Never paste unpublished manuscripts, personal data, or anyone else's private writing into an AI tool.**

This includes:
- Your novel draft, chapters, or scenes
- Character sheets with real names or addresses
- Your email, address, phone number, or ID numbers
- Someone else's writing that you do not own
- Medical, financial, or legal documents

## Why

When you type into a web-based AI chat, your text may be:
- Stored on the company's servers
- Reviewed by human trainers
- Used to train future models
- Leaked in a data breach

Once your text is out there, you cannot take it back. An unpublished manuscript that leaks is hard or impossible to protect.

## What is safe to paste

Generally safe, with common sense:
- Book blurbs you already published
- Publicly available text (for example, a back-cover copy you wrote and posted online)
- Made-up examples (fake character names, fake towns)
- Outlines and plot summaries that do not reveal your manuscript
- Your own already-published writing

When in doubt, do not paste it.

## Local models are more private

A local model runs on your computer. Your text does not leave your machine. This is safer for drafts, but treat it as "safer," not "perfect." Your computer can still be stolen, hacked, or broken. Keep backups offline.

## Education only

This guide is education only. It is not legal advice. If you are worried about copyright, contracts, or privacy law, talk to a qualified attorney.

## Checklist before you paste

- [ ] Is this my unpublished manuscript? If yes, do not paste it.
- [ ] Does this contain my real name, address, phone, or email? If yes, do not paste it.
- [ ] Does this contain someone else's private writing? If yes, do not paste it.
- [ ] Am I using a public web chat? If yes, assume anything you type could be stored.
- [ ] Do I have a backup of my work offline? If no, make one first.

## If you already pasted something

Do not panic. Delete the chat if the tool allows it. Do not paste more. Move forward using local tools or made-up examples only.
