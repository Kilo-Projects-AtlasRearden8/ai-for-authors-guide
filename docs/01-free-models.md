# Free AI Models

You do not need to pay for AI to write and publish. Capable free models exist, and this chapter shows you where to find them and how to use them.

## What "free model" means

An AI model is the program behind the chatbot. Some companies charge monthly subscriptions. Others offer free tiers or fully free models you can run yourself.

## Free options for authors

| Option | Cost | Best for | Notes |
|--------|------|----------|-------|
| Web chatbots with free tiers | Free (limited) | Drafting, editing, blurbs | You sign in with an email; usage limits apply |
| Local models (run on your computer) | Free | Privacy-sensitive work | Runs offline; needs a decent computer |
| Open-weight models | Free | Download and run locally | Released by researchers and companies |

### Web chatbots (easiest to start)

Popular AI chat services usually have a free tier. You open a website or app, create an account, and start typing. Free tiers often limit how many messages you can send per day or which model you can use.

**How to start:**
1. Pick a service with a free tier (your choice — this guide does not endorse any specific company).
2. Create an account with an email address.
3. Start a new chat and type your prompt.

### Local models (most private)

A local model runs on your own computer. Nothing you type leaves your machine. This is the best option for working on unpublished manuscripts, though Chapter 02 still applies.

Tools that make local models easy for beginners include free apps with graphical interfaces. You download the app, choose a model, download it once, and chat offline afterward.

**Trade-off:** local models need disk space (several gigabytes per model) and a computer with enough memory. They may be slower than web chatbots.

## How to pick a model

For writing and editing, look for a model described as good at:
- Following instructions
- Creative writing
- Summarizing and editing text

You do not need the biggest, most expensive model. A mid-sized free model is plenty for blurbs, drafting help, and editing.

## Quick start exercise

1. Open a free AI chat tool.
2. Type: "I am writing a novel. Can you help me brainstorm three opening scenes for a cozy mystery set in a small-town library?"
3. Read the answers. Pick the parts you like. Throw away the rest.

You are the author. AI is an assistant, not a replacement.

## Remember

Even with free tools: **never paste unpublished manuscripts or personal data.** Read Chapter 02 next.
