Blurb Planning Worksheet

AI for Authors · Asdale Dynamics, LLC

Print this page and fill it in by hand before you ask an AI tool for blurb ideas. Never paste unpublished manuscript text into an AI tool.

1. Main character (name + one sentence)

2. What does the main character want?

3. What stands in their way?

4. What happens if they fail? (the stakes)

5. One-sentence hook

6. What must NOT be revealed (spoilers to avoid)

Draft blurb

Take your answers to Chapter 05 and ask an AI tool for three blurb options. Rewrite your favorite in your own voice.
