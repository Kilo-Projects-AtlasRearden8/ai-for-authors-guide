Writing Blurbs

A blurb is the short description on your back cover or Amazon page. Its job is to make a reader want to buy the book. Most authors hate writing them. AI can help — but you must shape the final copy.

What a blurb needs

  1. Hook — grab attention in the first sentence
  2. Character — who is this about, and what do they want?
  3. Conflict — what stands in their way?
  4. Stakes — what happens if they fail?
  5. Tone — match the genre

What a blurb does not need

The whole plot, every character, or the ending — never spoil the ending.

Step 1: Gather your ingredients

Before you ask AI, write down: protagonist name and one-sentence description, what they want, what stands in their way, what happens if they fail, and genre and tone.

Step 2: Ask AI for options

Try this prompt:

"Write three back-cover blurb options for my [genre] book. Each blurb: 100–150 words, includes a hook, the main character, the central conflict, and the stakes. Do not reveal the ending. Match a [tone] tone. Book details: [your ingredients from Step 1]."

Read all three. Pick the pieces you like from each.

Step 3: Rewrite in your voice

AI blurbs often sound generic. Rewrite your chosen option so it sounds like you. Read it out loud. If you would not say it, do not print it.

Step 4: Test it

Show the blurb to a few readers who enjoy your genre. Ask: "Does this make you want to read the book?" Revise based on their answers.

Example (made-up)

Ingredients: Mara, a librarian who finds a hidden room. Wants to learn what the room is for. The town council wants to seal it. Stakes: a town secret that could destroy her family's reputation. Genre: cozy mystery, warm tone.

Librarian Mara Bell loves quiet. Then she finds a door no one else can see, leading to a room the town has forgotten. The council wants it sealed forever. Mara wants answers. The truth inside could clear her family's name — or ruin them all.

Remember: never paste your unpublished manuscript into an AI tool just to get a blurb. Use made-up details or your ingredients list instead. AI suggests. You decide. Your name is on the cover.
