Drafting with AI

The blank page is the hardest part. AI can help you start — but you remain the author. AI suggests. You decide.

The golden rule of drafting

Never let AI write your book for you. Use AI to brainstorm, outline, and get unstuck. Your voice, your characters, your story.

Step 1: Brainstorm a premise

Start small. Ask AI for ideas, not finished text.

Try this prompt:

"I want to write a [genre] novel. Give me five one-sentence story ideas. Keep them simple. Do not write full plots."

Read the ideas. Circle the one that excites you. Ignore the rest.

Step 2: Build a simple outline

You do not need a 50-page outline. You need enough to know what happens next.

Try this prompt:

"I am writing a [genre] story about [one-sentence idea]. Create a simple chapter-by-chapter outline with 10 chapters. For each chapter, give a one-sentence goal. Do not write dialogue or scenes."

Review the outline. Move chapters around. Delete what does not work. Add what is missing.

Step 3: Beat the blank page

When you are ready to write a chapter, use AI for a starting point, not the finished product.

Try this prompt:

"I am writing chapter 3 of my [genre] novel. The goal of this chapter is [one sentence]. My main character is [one-sentence description]. Give me three different opening paragraph options, each under 100 words. Do not finish the chapter."

Pick the option you like best. Rewrite it in your own voice. Then write the rest yourself.

Step 4: Get unstuck

Stuck on a scene? Ask for options, not answers.

Try this prompt:

"My character needs to [goal] in this scene, but I am stuck. Give me three ways the scene could go wrong, in one sentence each."

Pick one. Write it yourself.

Step 5: Keep your voice

AI writing often sounds generic or overly polite. That is fine — you will fix it in editing. For now, focus on getting words on the page.

Exercise

  1. Open your AI tool.
  2. Brainstorm three story ideas.
  3. Pick one and make a 10-chapter outline.
  4. Write one opening paragraph yourself, using an AI option only if you are stuck.

What to avoid

  • Do not paste your unpublished manuscript.
  • Do not ask AI to "write my novel."
  • Do not trust AI for facts. It makes things up. Verify anything you put in your book.
