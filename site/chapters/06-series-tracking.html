Series Tracking

If you write more than one book in the same world, you need a series bible: one place for characters, timelines, places, and world rules. AI helps you build and search it — but you keep the master record.

What goes in a series bible

  • Characters: names, ages, appearances, relationships, goals
  • Places: towns, buildings, landmarks
  • Timeline: when events happen, in what order
  • World rules: magic, technology, history
  • Objects: important items and what they do

Step 1: Pick where to keep it

Use something you control and can back up: a folder of text files on your computer, a notebook or binder, or a notes app with offline access. Do not keep your only series bible inside an AI chat.

Step 2: Build it one entry at a time

Start with your main character. Add entries as you write.

Name: Mara Bell
Age: 34
Role: Librarian, protagonist
Appearance: Brown hair in a bun, cardigan, always has a pen
Goal: Learn what the hidden room is for
Relationships: Sister Elena (estranged), coworker Sam (friend)
First appears: Book 1, Chapter 1

Step 3: Use AI to organize (safely)

You can paste a few entries you wrote yourself into AI and ask for help organizing them — but never paste unpublished scenes or full chapters.

Try this prompt:

"I am building a series bible. Here are some character and place entries I wrote. Check for contradictions: two characters with the same name, a place described two different ways, or timeline clashes. List only real problems. Entries: [paste your entries]"

Step 4: Update as you write

Every time you introduce a new character, place, or rule, add it to the series bible the same day.

Step 5: Use it while writing

Before you write a scene, check: does this character's age match earlier books? Is this place described consistently? Does this break a world rule? Fix it now.

Series tracking exercise

  1. Open a blank document or notebook.
  2. Create entries for your main character, your main setting, and one world rule.
  3. Paste those entries (not your manuscript) into AI and ask for contradiction checks.
  4. Save the document. Back it up.
