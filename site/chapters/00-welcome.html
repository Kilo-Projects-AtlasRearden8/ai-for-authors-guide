Welcome to AI for Authors

You wrote a book — or you want to. Maybe you're staring at a blank page. Maybe you have a messy first draft and no idea how to fix it. Maybe you hate writing blurbs. You're in the right place.

This guide walks you through using AI for writing and publishing, step by step, in plain English. It is written for beginners. You do not need to be a programmer. You do not need to spend money. Every tool in this guide has a free option.

Who this is for

This guide was written by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC. He explains AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

How to use this guide

  1. Read the chapters in order. Each one builds on the last.
  2. Try the exercises as you go. Open your AI tool and follow the steps.
  3. Print the worksheets and fill them out by hand.
  4. Keep this guide nearby while you write.
The one rule to remember. Education only. Never paste unpublished manuscripts, personal data, or anyone else's private writing into an AI tool. Read Chapter 02 before you paste anything.
