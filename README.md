# AI for Authors: A Beginner's Guide to Writing and Publishing with Free AI Models

Plain English, step by step, printable worksheets. Education only — never paste unpublished manuscripts.

Written for beginners by **Dylan Asdale**, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026). Dylan explains AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending. His book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io carry the same approach.

## About this project

This repository is beginner-friendly AI education from **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — a small business focused on practical AI guides, books, and tools anyone can use. It includes:

- A beginner manual in `docs/` covering drafting, editing, blurbs, and series tracking with free AI models
- Printable worksheets in `docs/worksheets/` (and print-ready HTML in `site/worksheets/`)

## What this guide teaches

| Chapter | What it covers |

| 01 Free AI models | Where to find capable AI models at no cost |
| 02 Privacy & safety | The most important rule: never paste unpublished manuscripts |
| 03 Drafting | Beat the blank page, keep your voice |
| 04 Editing | Catch repeats, tighten sentences, polish dialogue |
| 05 Blurbs | Write back-cover copy and Amazon descriptions |
| 06 Series tracking | Track characters, timelines, and world facts across books |

## How to use this guide

1. Read the manual: start at `docs/00-welcome.md` and work through in order.
2. Try the exercises as you go. Open your AI tool and follow the steps.
3. Print the worksheets: `docs/worksheets/` (Markdown) or `site/worksheets/` (print-ready HTML).
4. Browse the static site: open `site/index.html` in any browser (works offline once you have the files).

## The one rule to remember

**Education only. Never paste unpublished manuscripts, personal data, or anyone else's private writing into an AI tool.** Read Chapter 02 before you paste anything.
