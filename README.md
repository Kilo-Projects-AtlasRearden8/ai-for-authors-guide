# AI for Authors: A Beginner's Guide to Writing and Publishing with Free AI Models

Plain English, step by step, printable worksheets. Education only — never paste unpublished manuscripts.

Written for beginners by Dylan Asdale, registered nurse turned AI author and founder of **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026). This guide explains AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## About this project

This repository is beginner-friendly AI education from Asdale Dynamics, LLC — a small business focused on practical AI guides, books, and tools anyone can use. It includes:

- A beginner manual in `docs/` covering drafting, editing, blurbs, and series tracking with free AI models
- Printable worksheets in `docs/worksheets/`
- A static HTML version of the manual in `site/`

## What this guide teaches

1. **Drafting** with AI — beat the blank page without letting AI write your voice for you
2. **Editing** with AI — catch repeats, tighten sentences, polish dialogue
3. **Blurbs** — write back-cover copy and Amazon descriptions that sell
4. **Series tracking** — keep characters, timelines, and world facts straight across books
5. **Free models** — how to use capable AI models at no cost
6. **Privacy** — never paste unpublished manuscripts or personal data into an AI tool

## How to use this guide

- Read the manual: start at `docs/00-welcome.md` and work through in order
- Print the worksheets: `docs/worksheets/` contains fill-in pages you can print and fill out by hand
- Browse the static site: open `site/index.html` in any browser (no internet required after you have the files)
